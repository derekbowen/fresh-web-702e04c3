## 0. Answer in one page

**What is not the cause. [V]** The URLs we advertise are technically healthy. Of the 10,074
distinct URLs in the live sitemaps, 10,072 return 200, 2 return 301, 1 is noindex, none
carries an `X-Robots-Tag`, and none returned a 5xx. Across 13,482 fetches in this audit
there was no 5xx at all. `robots.txt` on `www.poolrentalnearme.com` does not block any
sitemap URL. Google is not being locked out.

**What the numbers say. [V + I]** Google knows about 32,067 URLs and we advertise 10,074.
At least 21,993 of the URLs Google knows are ones we do not advertise. We generate most of
them ourselves: indexable pages link to 10,136 distinct URLs that are in no sitemap, and
8,986 of those are `noindex` by design. That is larger than the whole "Excluded by noindex"
bucket (7,847).

**Why indexed pages are falling. [I]** The two buckets that grew while the index shrank are
the quality ones: "Duplicate, Google chose different canonical" went 2,463 (Sep 1) to 2,987,
and "Crawled, currently not indexed" went 1,956 to 2,856. The site matches both:

- 3,617 of 3,932 host-acquisition city pages state that the city has no pools. Since
  2026-09-18 that statement sits above the page body. The first index cliff is dated
  around Sep 21.
- 1,091 comparison pages share one template (median 5-word overlap 0.72 and 0.64), 156 of
  them are exact text duplicates of another, and 1,088 have no internal link pointing at them.
- 1,587 `/p/` pages share an exact H1 with another page; 124 city pairs have the same H1 on
  a guest page and a host page.
- 1,679 of 2,000 indexed public-pool pages print a raw JSON array in the body, and 829
  contain first-person filler such as "My information doesn't specify".

**What could not be established. [U]** Which exact URLs Google dropped. Search Console
URL-level data is not reachable from this session (the `gsc_*` tables are empty and no
Search Console connector is authorised). Section 12 lists the exports that close this gap.
Nothing in the records explains a second cliff near Oct 19.

## 8. `/s` end-state (Q8)

Today **[V]**: bare `/s` is indexable; every `/s?…` is `noindex,follow`; `/s/<path>` is 404.
Indexable pages link to 3,641 distinct `/s?…` URLs (3,621 of them `?address=`).

Recommended end-state (Derek decides):

1. Bare `/s` stays indexable, self-canonical, and goes into `sitemap-static.xml`.
2. Every `/s?…` stays `noindex,follow`. No city search page becomes indexable; city intent
   belongs to `/p/<city>-<st>`.
3. Templates stop emitting a crawlable `/s?address=<city>` link on cities with no pools.
   On those pages the link goes to bare `/s`. Cities with pools keep their listing cards.
4. The robots pattern is widened to cover `/s?page=`.

`/s` is served by the WEST marketplace. Items 1, 2 and 4 are already true or are static
edits; item 3 is an EAST template change. A fresh-web `/s` mirror exists as unfinished work
(`c02aee4`); if `/s` ever moves to EAST the same rules must move with it.

## 13. Ranked fix plan

Ordered by expected index effect divided by risk. Nothing here is done. Each item waits for
its own GO. "WEST" items touch the marketplace box and are flagged for that reason.

### F0. Get the Search Console URL lists (section 12)

- **Risk:** none. **Why first:** it turns sections 3 and 4 from inference into fact and
  tells us which of F1, F6, F7 and F8 matters most.

### F1. Take the "no pools here" notice off the top of city pages with no inventory

- **Problem:** about 3,600 host-acquisition pages open with a statement that the city has
  nothing, placed above the body since Sep 18.
- **Evidence:** 3,617 of 3,932 pages match; component and placement confirmed in code;
  date from the Sep 18 record.
- **Where:** EAST. `src/components/templates/host-acq-city.tsx` (section order, line ~467),
  `src/components/live-inventory.tsx` (empty state, lines ~189 to 214).
- **Change:** when a city has no eligible pool, render no inventory section on the
  host-intent template. Cities with pools keep the section where it is.
- **Expected GSC effect:** slows or reverses growth of "Crawled, not indexed" on the city
  family over 4 to 8 weeks. **[I]**
- **Risk:** low. One template, no data change, reversible by one commit. EAST build and
  pm2 restart as ubuntu.
- **Verify:** re-crawl the 3,932 URLs; the phrase count goes from 3,617 to 0; pages with
  pools still show cards; H1 and canonical unchanged.

### F2. Stop linking indexable pages to noindex URLs; retire the noindex sitemap

- **Problem:** templates emit 3,088 distinct `/p/pool-pros?city=` links and 3,621
  `/s?address=` links, all to `noindex` pages, and `/sitemap-directory.xml` advertises
  1,357 more.
- **Evidence:** stage-2 crawl of 3,408 non-sitemap link targets.
- **Where:** EAST. `src/components/templates/host-acq-city.tsx`, `activity-city.tsx`,
  `advocacy.tsx`, `src/routes/p.pool-rentals-{$state}.tsx`, the two comparison routes,
  `src/routes/sitemap-directory[.]xml.ts`.
- **Change:** pool-pros link without the `?city=` parameter, or removed; `/s?address=`
  link replaced by bare `/s` on cities with no pools (section 8); `sitemap-directory`
  route answers 410.
- **Expected GSC effect:** "Excluded by noindex" stops growing and falls over months. It
  does not add indexed pages by itself; it stops spending crawl on about 8,000 dead ends.
- **Risk:** low. Users lose a pre-filled search link on empty cities.
- **Verify:** re-run `links.py`; distinct noindex link targets drop from 8,986 to the
  1,366 withheld public-pool pages.

### F3. A failed database read must answer 503, never 404 or 301

- **Problem:** a Supabase error turns a live page into a 404, or a 301 to a generic page,
  and the edge caches it for 10 minutes.
- **Evidence:** code path in `lookupContentPage`; 58 live pages logged as 404 on Sep 16 and
  193 on Jul 15.
- **Where:** EAST. `lookupContentPage` in `src/server/content-pages.functions.ts` (line ~124)
  and its caller `src/routes/p.$slug.tsx`; the sub-sitemap routes
